Legal Scale LLP - SMS Privacy Policy and Terms

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Legal Scale LLP (Hoboken, New Jersey area) operates an internal SMS assistant line at (201) 630-7708. The line is used only by Legal Scale personnel who opt in to text the firm's AI assistant and receive replies, confirmations and reminders. It is not used for marketing or public messaging.

SMS Privacy Policy

Information we collect

When you use the assistant line we collect your mobile phone number, the content of messages you send and receive, and message metadata such as dates and times.

How we use it

We use this information only to provide the assistant service: responding to your messages, sending confirmations and reminders you request, and maintaining your opt-in status.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be processed by service providers (such as our SMS carrier and messaging platform) solely to deliver the service, or disclosed where required by law.

Retention

We keep phone numbers, message content and opt-in records only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal and professional record-keeping obligations, then delete them. After you opt out, we retain only the record of your opt-out and consent history.

Contact

Questions about this policy: Neil@legalscale.com
