Legal Scale LLP SMS Terms of Service

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Program

Legal Scale LLP Client Text Messages. Legal Scale LLP, a law firm in Hoboken, NJ, sends text messages from (201) 630-7708 to current clients who opt in, about their own legal matters: matter status updates, reminders for meetings, calls and document signings, and requests for documents or information. Clients may also text us with questions. We do not send marketing or promotional messages.

How to opt in

  • Checking the optional, unchecked SMS consent box in our client intake form or engagement letter.
  • Texting START to (201) 630-7708.
  • Giving verbal consent to firm staff by phone or in person, which is recorded in the client file.

Consent is not a condition of representation. Details: SMS Opt-In.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply.

Help and opt-out

Text HELP to (201) 630-7708 for help.

Text STOP to (201) 630-7708 at any time to opt out. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages. Text START to opt back in.
