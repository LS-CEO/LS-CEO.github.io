How Legal Scale LLP clients opt in to text messages

Text messages from Legal Scale LLP are optional. Clients may decline and still receive full representation.

1. Client intake form / engagement letter

Our intake form and engagement letter include the following optional checkbox, which is unchecked by default:

Mobile number: ____________________

2. Text START

Clients may text START to (201) 630-7708. They receive this confirmation before any other message:

Legal Scale LLP: You're subscribed to texts about your legal matter (updates, reminders, document requests). Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

3. Verbal consent (phone or in person)

Firm staff read this script:

"Would you like to receive text messages from Legal Scale LLP about your matter, such as updates, appointment and signing reminders, and document requests? Message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP anytime to opt out or HELP for help. Saying yes is optional."

Consent is recorded in the client's file with the date and time only if the client says yes.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop messages, or HELP for help. See the SMS Terms of Service.
