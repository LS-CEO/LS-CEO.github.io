Legal Scale LLP Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how Legal Scale LLP ("Legal Scale," "we"), a law firm in Hoboken, New Jersey, handles information connected with our client text messaging program at (201) 630-7708.

Information we collect

When a client opts in to text messages we collect the client's mobile phone number, the content of messages sent and received, message dates and times, and a record of the client's consent (method, date and time) and any opt-out.

How we use it

We use this information only to communicate with clients about their own legal matters: matter status updates, reminders for scheduled meetings, calls and document signings, requests for documents or information, and replies to client questions, and to maintain each client's opt-in status.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information may be processed by service providers (such as our SMS carrier and messaging platform) solely to deliver messages, or disclosed where required by law or professional rules.

Confidentiality

Text messages do not include confidential case details. Substantive legal advice is provided by phone, in person, or through the firm's established secure channels.

Retention

We keep phone numbers, message records and consent records as long as needed to serve the client and meet legal and professional record-keeping obligations. After an opt-out we retain the opt-out and consent history.

Contact

Questions: (201) 630-7708 or Neil@legalscale.com. See also our SMS Terms of Service.
